Device matching and spoken requests are still being tested. Keep your usual controls and assistant available.

Your home, in your words. Runs locally on your Home Assistant hub, without a cloud service or LLM.

Get ready

  1. Install the Assist integration below, then restart Home Assistant once.
  2. In Settings → Devices & services, add Pixora Voice.
  3. In Settings → Voice assistants, select Pixora Voice as the conversation agent, with local speech recognition and Piper for speech.
  4. Assign each voice satellite to its room and expose the entities you want to use in Assist.

On Yellow, start with local Speech-to-Phrase. Its recognized sentences must include the questions you want to ask. The app does not use your browser's speech service.

Checking setup…

Only the app’s own integration folder is installed. Existing versions are backed up locally. This does not restart HA or change your current voice pipeline.

Try a home question

This sends real device commands when you ask for them. For “in here,” talk through a satellite assigned to a room.

Try saying

  • Is the front door open?
  • Are there any windows open?
  • Is the heat on?
  • What’s the temperature in here?
  • Turn on the ceiling light.
  • Turn off all kitchen lights.

Open/closed uses contact sensors; locked/unlocked uses locks. Heating activity comes from the thermostat’s current action. Ambiguous requests ask which device you meant.

How your controller works

Schedules repeat weekly in Home Assistant's time zone. Each period stays active until the next one. An override has a clear end time; changes made at the physical thermostat create a temporary hold.

When selected contacts remain open for your chosen delay, heating is lowered to your setback and cooling is paused. After all contacts close and the return delay passes, the current schedule or unexpired override resumes. Outdoor temperatures above your limit turn heat off; a return margin prevents repeated switching at the threshold.

Protection stays active during overrides. Missing readings pause new changes. A command with an uncertain result is never retried automatically. Disabling a controller leaves the thermostat at its current settings. Configure equipment limits and freeze protection on the thermostat itself. Avoid running another schedule controller on the same thermostat.
